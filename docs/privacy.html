English

*Prayer Times* ("the app") calculates Islamic prayer times on your device, plays the adhan and shows notifications and a home-screen widget.

*We do not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.* The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

  • *No internet access.* The app does not request the Internet permission. Prayer times are calculated on your device from the region you choose. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • *No location access.* You choose your city / region manually in Settings. The app never reads your GPS or network location.
  • *Settings stay on your device.* Your choices (region, colors, text size, messages you type, time adjustments, language, etc.) are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Deleting the app or its data removes them.
  • *Files you choose.* If you pick a wallpaper image or an audio file, the app reads it through Android's file picker and keeps only a reference on your device. The file is not copied, uploaded or shared.
  • *Permissions and why.*
  • Notifications — to show the prayer-time message and the Pause/Stop buttons while the adhan plays.
  • Alarms (exact alarms) — to start the adhan at the exact prayer time, even when the app is closed.
  • Foreground service (media playback) — to keep the adhan playing when the screen is off or the app is closed.
  • Run at start-up — to set the next prayer alarm again after the device restarts.
  • Keep the device awake (wake lock) — so the adhan is not cut off while it plays.
  • *Children.* The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.
  • *Changes.* If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
  • *Contact.* For any question, write to [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].
